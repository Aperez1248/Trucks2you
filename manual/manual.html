What Trucks2U does

Trucks2U connects paving and grading contractors with vetted dump truck owner-operators (10 wheelers, 12 wheelers and super dumpers). It works like Uber for hauling: a contractor posts a job, every approved truck owner with the right type of truck gets the request on their phone, and the first ones to accept get the spots. During the day the app tracks every load, collects the scale tickets, and at the end of the day the contractor approves the hours and everyone gets paid by bank transfer.

Trucks2U charges a 5% fee, built into the hourly rate. A typical broker takes 10 to 12%.

How one job moves through the app

1PostContractor enters the site, plant, trucks and rate.
2RequestVetted owners with the right trucks get it on their phone.
3AcceptFirst to tap Accept get the spots. The rest disappear.
4HaulDrivers check in, haul, and snap each scale ticket.
5ApproveContractor reviews hours and tickets, taps Approve.
6Get paidContractor billed by ACH. Owners paid in about 2 days.

The four kinds of users

Contractor

Posts jobs, plans how many trucks are needed, watches trucks live, shares a tracking link with the customer, approves the day and pays.

Truck owner / driver

Signs up and uploads documents once, accepts job requests, reads dispatch updates, checks in, records load tickets, assigns drivers to trucks and gets paid weekly.

Broker

Dispatches their own trucks to their own customers through the app instead of calling and texting. Pays Trucks2U a flat fee per truck per day.

Admin (Trucks2U office)

Reviews every new owner's documents before they can see jobs, watches for expiring insurance and medical cards, and keeps the plant directory up to date.

Getting around the prototype

  3. Back links in blue at the top left of a screen take you back one step.

Part 1

Contractor

Post a job, get trucks without phone calls, watch every load, and pay once at the end of the day.

1Your jobs

The Jobs tab lists every job you have posted, newest first. Each card shows:

  • The date and start time.
  • A status: Finding trucks while spots are open, Ready to approve when every truck has checked out.
  • A progress bar and chips like 12W 2/3, meaning 2 of the 3 twelve-wheelers you asked for are booked.

Tap a card to open the job. Tap + Post a job to start a new one.

2Post a job: job details and plant

Posting takes three short steps. On step 1 you fill in:

  • Job name, for example "Elm St mill and pave".
  • What the trucks are doing: haul off grindings, deliver asphalt, or a round trip (grindings out, asphalt in).
  • Job site address, with cross streets.
  • Asphalt plant. The list is sorted closest first and shows the miles and rough drive time for each plant, so picking the nearest one is one tap. Recycling yards are marked.
  • Notes for drivers: staging spot, safety gear, gate codes. You can add more detail later in the job's dispatch info.

Tap Next.

3Load planner: how many trucks do I need?

Step 2 starts with a calculator so you don't have to guess. Enter:

  • Tons to move and hours of work.
  • One-way drive time to the plant (filled in from the plant you picked).
  • Load and dump time per trip.

The app works out the round trip time and how many loads each truck can do in the day, then tells you how many trucks of each type it takes. In the example, 600 tons with a 16 minute drive needs 3 twelve-wheelers.

Tap Use next to the option you want and the counts below are set for you. You can still change each count with − and +, and set the hourly rate for each truck type. Tap Recalculate after changing a number.

4Schedule, review and post

Step 3 asks for the date and start time, then shows a review of the whole job with the trucks, the rates, and an estimate for a 10 hour day.

The review also shows the Trucks2U fee that is already inside that estimate (5%) next to what a broker would take (11%), so you can see the savings.

Tap Post job to vetted owners.

Who gets it? Only truck owners who have passed vetting and have the right type of truck. Owners with expired insurance or paperwork don't see it.

5Watch the trucks accept

As soon as you post, the job opens and the app tells you how many vetted drivers it was sent to. Trucks start appearing in the Trucks list as owners accept, and a message pops up each time ("R&J Trucking accepted, 2 of 3 filled").

It's first come, first served. Once every spot is filled, the request disappears from everyone else's phone.

See what drivers see switches to the truck owner view so you can see the request from the other side.

Prototype note: the "other drivers" accepting are simulated. They stop with one spot left, so you can try accepting it yourself from the Truck owner view.

6The job during the day

Open any job to see everything in one place:

  • Route from the job site to the plant, and the dispatch info for drivers (see the next step).
  • Loads, tons hauled and how many trucks are on the clock right now.
  • Trucks: each truck, its owner, when it checked in, and its status (Assigned, On the clock, Checked out).
  • Load tickets: every scale ticket a driver has recorded, with the ticket number, time, tons and a photo.

Share a tracking link with your customer

Tap Share tracking link to copy a web link (like trucks2u.com/track/j-1042) you can text to the property owner or GC. They see the trucks and arrival times without downloading anything or logging in.

See trucks on the map opens the Live map.

7Dispatch info for drivers

Every job has a Dispatch info for drivers card, right under the route. It holds what drivers need to find the job and work it safely:

  • On-site contact and contact phone.
  • Staging spot, and gate and access (gate codes, which street to come in on).
  • Plant order or ticket number and the material or mix.
  • Safety gear and any other notes.

Tap Edit to fill in or change any of these. When you tap Save and notify drivers, every driver on the job gets a text and an alert in the app telling them the info changed.

Why it matters: drivers stop calling the foreman for the gate code or the order number. It's all on their phone, on the job screen.
The dispatch info card on a contractor's job

8Send an update to every driver

When something changes during the day (the plant is down, the paver slowed, a street closed), type it in Send an update to every driver on this job and tap Send. The button shows how many drivers will get it.

Each driver gets a text and an alert in the app. Your updates are listed newest first, and each one shows Read by 1 of 4, so you know who has seen it and who you still need to call.

One message replaces calling or texting every driver one by one.

A new update, with how many drivers have read each one

9Live map: where are my trucks?

The Live map tab shows today's job with every truck moving between the job site and the plant.

  • The green circles are geofences around the site and the plant. When a truck drives in or out, the arrival and departure are logged on their own, so cycle times and wait times are recorded without anyone typing anything.
  • Below the map, each truck shows what it is doing right now (loading, hauling to the plant with an ETA, returning) and how many loads it has done.
  • The average cycle time is shown at the top of the list.
  • A truck that hasn't checked in by start time is flagged Late?
Prototype note: the truck movement is simulated. In the real app each truck's position comes from the driver's phone every 30 seconds.

10Approve the day

When every truck has checked out, the job card says Ready to approve. Open the job and scroll to Approve the day. You'll see each truck's hours times its rate, and the total.

Check the hours against the load tickets above it, then tap Approve hours and tickets. That one tap:

  • Sends you the invoice, paid by bank transfer (ACH).
  • Starts the payouts to every truck owner on the job.

The total already includes the 5% Trucks2U fee, and the screen shows what a broker would have taken for comparison.

Bottom of a finished job

11Billing

The Billing tab shows:

  • Approved this month, the Trucks2U fee inside it, and how much you kept compared with an 11% broker.
  • Waiting on your approval: finished jobs you still need to approve. Tap one to review it.
  • Invoices for jobs you've approved.
After approving the Lakeside job

12Asphalt plant directory

The Plants tab lists 19 Bay Area hot mix plants and recycling yards. It combines the Caltrans approved plant list (September 2025) with your own plant list.

  • Distance from: pick a city (Oakland, San Jose, Concord and others) and the list re-sorts closest first, with miles and drive time.
  • Search by plant name or city.
  • Tags show the plant type, Caltrans approved, Hours listed, or Recycling yard.

The same list feeds the plant picker when you post a job.

Plant details

Tap a plant to see its address, distance, loaded drive time, phone and sales numbers, published hours, and whether it takes grindings (RAP).

Wait at scale fills in on its own once Trucks2U trucks start checking in and out at that plant, so over time you'll know which plants are backed up.

If something is wrong or missing, tap Add hours or grindings info. Your correction goes to the Trucks2U office to check before it's added.

Prototype note: map pins are approximate, and hours and grindings acceptance still need to be confirmed with each plant.
Part 2

Truck owner and driver

Sign up once, get job requests on your phone, and get paid in about 2 business days instead of 30 or more.

1Signing up and uploading your documents

A new owner signs up from the Docs tab ("Sign up and upload your documents"). It takes about 10 minutes on a phone. Each document can be a photo taken right in the app or a PDF. Nothing is shared until you tap Send for review, and you can stop and finish later. There are five steps:

StepWhat you enter or upload
1. Business and DOTBusiness name, your name, mobile phone, email, USDOT number (tap Check to confirm it's active with the federal FMCSA database), MC or CA number if you have one.
2. Driver's licenseLicense class and expiration, photos of the front and back, and the DOT medical card.
3. InsuranceCertificate of insurance (COI) listing Trucks2U as additional insured, auto liability limit, expiration date, and workers' comp certificate or the sole-owner exemption.
4. Your truckTruck type, license plate, VIN, registration, annual DOT inspection report, and a side photo of the truck with the plate showing.
5. Testing, taxes and payoutDrug and alcohol testing program letter, W-9, connecting your bank for payouts, and consent to a driving record (MVR) check and FMCSA Clearinghouse query.
Step 1: the USDOT check
Step 2: license and medical card photos
Step 3: insurance
Step 4: truck details and photos
Step 5: testing, W-9, bank and consent
Sent. Most owners are approved within 1 business day.
Bank details are safe: owners connect their bank through the payment partner, so Trucks2U never sees the account number. If something is missing on step 5, the app lists exactly what is still needed and the Send button stays grey.

2Today: new job requests

The Today tab is the home screen. New job requests that fit your trucks show up at the top with a blue border. Each one shows:

  • Date, start time, the route and the type of work.
  • How many spots are still open.
  • Each of your trucks that fits, with the hourly rate and about what you'll make for a 10 hour day.

Tap Accept on the truck you want to send. Tap Pass to hide the request.

First come, first served. If another driver takes the last spot first, you'll see "Sorry, another driver just took that spot."

Below the requests are your verified status, any warning like Insurance expires in 24 days, and the jobs your trucks are booked on.

After you accept

The request moves down into your booked jobs with the status Assigned, and the contractor sees your truck on their job right away.

Tap a booked job to open it on the day.

3Find work

The Find work tab lists every open job that needs one of your trucks, even ones you passed on the Today screen.

Each line shows the contractor's rate and what you actually get after the 5% fee. In the example, $130 an hour means you get $123.50 an hour.

4Check in at the site

When the truck gets to the job, open the job and tap Check in at site. That records the time and location and puts the truck On the clock.

In the full app check-in happens on its own when the truck drives into the job site geofence.

5Dispatch info and updates from dispatch

Every job screen has a Dispatch info card with the on-site contact and phone, staging spot, gate and access, plant order number, mix, safety gear and notes.

When dispatch sends an update or changes the info, the driver gets a text and an alert. In the app:

  • The job card on the Today tab shows a tag like 2 new dispatch notes.
  • On the job screen the card has an orange border, and new updates are marked New in bold.
  • Tap Got it to let dispatch know you've read them. The marks go away, and the contractor or broker sees you've read it.
Two new updates waiting for the driver

After tapping Got it

The updates stay on the card as a record of the day, with the time each one was sent, and the dispatch info stays underneath for the driver to check any time.

Read: no more "New" marks
The "new dispatch notes" tag on the Today tab

6Load tickets and checking out

For every load, the driver adds the scale ticket:

  1. Type the ticket number and tons.
  2. Snap a photo of the ticket.
  3. Tap Save ticket. The contractor sees it right away.

That replaces the paper tickets that get lost in the cab.

At the end of the day, enter the hours on the clock and tap Check out. The day goes to the contractor to approve. Your tickets at the bottom shows every load and the total tons.

7Fleet: trucks and drivers

Owners with more than one truck or driver use the Fleet tab.

  • For each truck, pick who is driving it and tap Save. That driver gets the job texts and checks in from their own phone.
  • You see every load your drivers haul, and you get paid for the truck.
  • Each driver goes through the same license, medical card, MVR and drug testing checks and shows Verified when cleared.

8Pay

The Pay tab shows:

  • Paid this month, money on the way, and money awaiting approval from contractors.
  • Recent work with what each day paid.
  • Weekly statements broken out by truck, with days and loads.

Payouts land within 2 business days of the contractor approving the day.

9Docs: keeping everything current

The Docs tab is a checklist of every document on file: USDOT, auto liability, general liability and workers' comp, CDL and MVR, medical card, drug testing, each truck's registration and inspection, and the W-9 and bank.

  • A green check means it's good. An orange ! means it expires soon.
  • Every item has to stay green for your trucks to see jobs.
  • The app reminds you 30 days before anything expires.

To renew insurance, choose the new certificate under Renew insurance certificate and tap Upload. Trucks2U reviews it within 1 business day.

Part 3

Broker

Dispatch your own trucks to your own customers without phone calls or group texts, and fill open Trucks2U jobs with your trucks.

1Dispatch board

The Dispatch tab has two lists.

Your customers' jobs (top): jobs from the broker's own customers, with how many trucks still need to be dispatched, like 2 to dispatch.

Open jobs from Trucks2U contractors (bottom): jobs posted in the app that still need trucks. A broker can fill these with their trucks.

Tap any job to dispatch trucks to it.

2Dispatching your own customer's job

The job is split by truck type. For each open spot:

  1. Pick a truck from the list (only your free, verified trucks are shown).
  2. Tap Dispatch. The driver gets the job details by text right away.

Tap Remove to take a truck off a job. The driver is told.

Fee: Trucks2U charges the broker a flat fee per truck dispatched. Your rates and billing with your customer stay the same.

3Dispatch info for your drivers

The broker's job screen has the same Dispatch info for drivers card as the contractor's. Tap Edit to add your customer's on-site contact, staging spot, gate code, safety gear and notes, then Save and notify drivers.

Use Send an update to every driver on this job for changes during the day. Your drivers get it by text, so it replaces the group text.

4Filling a Trucks2U contractor job

Open a job from the "Open jobs from Trucks2U contractors" list. It shows the open spots and the contractor's rate.

Pick one of your trucks and tap Dispatch. The contractor sees the truck on their job right away, listed under the owner's name "via" your company.

On these jobs the normal 5% fee applies instead of the per-truck fee, and the contractor pays through the app.

5Your trucks

The Trucks tab lists the owner-operators you dispatch, whether they're working or free today, and their vetting status.

  • Verified: ready to dispatch.
  • Expiring: a document runs out soon.
  • Blocked: a document is out of date, so the truck can't be dispatched until it's fixed.

Every truck goes through the same Trucks2U vetting. Invite a truck owner texts an owner a sign-up link.

6Billing

Shows trucks dispatched this month, the fee per truck, and what is owed to Trucks2U. It's billed monthly by bank transfer, and a truck counts once per day it's dispatched.

Not decided yet: the $15 per truck per day fee is a placeholder until the real price is set.
Part 4

Admin (Trucks2U office)

Make sure every truck on the app is legal, insured and safe before it sees a single job.

1Vetting queue

Every owner who sends in a sign-up lands here. No owner sees jobs until every check is green.

Each applicant shows how many trucks they have, how many of the 8 checks have passed, and a tag: To review (documents waiting for you) or Failed (something was rejected).

Preview the sign-up form drivers fill in opens the same form owners see.

2Reviewing an applicant

Open an applicant to see every check with the uploaded document right under it.

  • Some checks run on their own, like the USDOT lookup. These show a green check already.
  • For the rest (insurance, workers' comp, CDL, medical card, drug testing, registration and inspection), look at the picture and the dates, then tap Mark OK.
  • If a document is wrong, blurry or expired, tap Reject. The owner gets a text asking for a new one.
Top of an applicant's review page

3Approving the owner

The green Approve owner and trucks button only turns on once every check is OK.

Tap it and the owner moves to Approved today. From that moment their trucks start getting job requests.

Every check OK, so Approve is on
The owner now shows as Approved

4Overview

A quick look at the business: trucks working today, open jobs, and Trucks2U fees this month.

Expiring in the next 45 days lists every insurance policy, medical card and inspection about to run out. Owners get reminders at 30, 14 and 3 days. On the expiration date their trucks stop seeing jobs until the new document is approved.

Plant directory upkeep

Corrections contractors send from a plant's page ("Add hours or grindings info") come to the office to check before they're added to the directory. The directory today is the Caltrans list merged with your plant list.

Coming after the pilot: a live map of every truck, geofence arrival alerts and utilization reports for the office.
